01BLine

The FIRST Robotics Competition is a game of autonomy.

Our robot, Team 2638, testing a BLine auto in the shop

Making a robot drive a precise path on its own is one of the hardest parts of the game, and most teams don’t have the tools to do it well.

Open source

BLine.

So I built one. BLine is a free, open-source tool that lets any team draw a path, simulate it, and run it on their robot.

Built for my team.
